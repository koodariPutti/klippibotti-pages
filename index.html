klippibotti

klippibotti is a personal tool that cuts short vertical video clips from source material whose owners have allowed clipping, adds captions, and uploads the finished clips to the developer's own YouTube channel as YouTube Shorts.

What it does

  • Creates clips only from material permitted by the rules of the campaign it is made for.
  • Uploads the clips to a single YouTube channel owned by the developer, using the YouTube Data API.
  • Is not offered to the public and does not process any other person's data.

klippibotti uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

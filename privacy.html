Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

klippibotti is a personal tool used by its developer to upload video clips to the developer's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and it has no other users.

Data the app accesses

The app requests one Google OAuth scope, youtube.upload, for the developer's own Google account. It uses this permission only to upload videos to that account's YouTube channel.

Data the app stores

The OAuth token that lets the app upload videos is stored only on the developer's own computer or server. The app does not collect, store or process personal data about any other person.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or disclose Google user data to anyone.

YouTube API Services

klippibotti uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the stored token from the machine where the app runs also ends the app's access.
